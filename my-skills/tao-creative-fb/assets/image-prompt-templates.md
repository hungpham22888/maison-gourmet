# Image Prompt Templates — Maison Gourmet

> Dùng file này để tạo prompt chuẩn cho gpt-image-1.
> Copy nguyên prompt base, thêm context cụ thể vào cuối.
> Luôn giữ phong cách: premium, sạch sẽ, ánh sáng đẹp, không chữ trên ảnh.

---

## PHONG CÁCH ẢNH CHUNG (luôn áp dụng)

**Style guidelines áp dụng cho mọi ảnh:**
- Nhiếp ảnh thực phẩm/quà tặng cao cấp, phong cách editorial
- Ánh sáng tự nhiên mềm hoặc studio sáng nhẹ, tông ấm
- Nền: trắng kem, beige, xám nhạt, hoặc cẩm thạch trắng
- Không có chữ, logo, watermark trong ảnh
- Tỉ lệ 1:1 (1024x1024)
- Phong cách: clean, minimal, elegant

---

## MODE 1 — ORGANIC CONTENT

### Template 1A: Showcase sản phẩm đơn giản
```
Professional product photography of a premium Vietnamese gift box, elegant packaging with warm gold accents, featuring a wine bottle and artisan food items arranged beautifully. Soft natural lighting from the left, cream white background, shallow depth of field. Shot from 45-degree angle. Clean, minimal, luxury aesthetic. No text, no logos. --ar 1:1
```

### Template 1B: Lifestyle / Tặng quà
```
Lifestyle photography of two people exchanging a beautifully wrapped premium gift box at a warmly lit office, Vietnamese business setting, both smiling naturally. The gift box has elegant dark packaging with gold ribbon. Warm afternoon light, bokeh background, candid moment. Premium editorial style. No text. --ar 1:1
```

### Template 1C: Flat lay mùa lễ
```
Elegant flat lay of a luxury Vietnamese gift hamper, including wine bottle, premium snacks, and artisan chocolates, arranged on a white marble surface with dried flowers and golden ribbon accents. Top-down view, soft shadows, warm tones. Minimalist luxury food photography style. No text. --ar 1:1
```

### Template 1D: Close-up chi tiết
```
Close-up macro photography of a handcrafted premium gift box lid being opened, revealing tissue paper-wrapped items inside, warm golden interior. Dramatic but soft lighting from above, bokeh effect, teal and gold color palette. High-end packaging photography. No text. --ar 1:1
```

### Template 1E: Mùa Tết
```
Tet holiday gift arrangement with a premium gift box featuring traditional Vietnamese elements, red and gold accents, cherry blossom branch, placed on a lacquered red tray. Festive but sophisticated. Warm studio lighting, white background. Luxury Vietnamese Tet aesthetic. No text. --ar 1:1
```

## MODE 2 — CREATIVE ADS

### Template 2A: Pain Point (Angle: khó chọn quà)
```
Frustrated professional woman at office desk surrounded by multiple open browser tabs showing gift options, looking overwhelmed and stressed. Modern Vietnamese office setting, late afternoon, laptop glow. Realistic editorial photography, slightly desaturated mood. Relatable emotion, not exaggerated. No text. --ar 1:1
```
> *Dùng cho angle: pain point — khó chọn quà, mất thời gian*

### Template 2B: Solution (Angle: Maison là giải pháp)
```
Confident Vietnamese HR manager handing a beautifully packaged Maison Gourmet gift box to a smiling colleague in a modern office lobby. The gift box is prominently featured with elegant dark packaging. Warm professional lighting, clean background. Editorial corporate photography. No text. --ar 1:1
```
> *Dùng cho angle: solution — có Maison Gourmet, mọi thứ đơn giản*

### Template 2C: Social Proof (Angle: nhiều người tin dùng)
```
Rows of elegantly identical premium gift boxes with dark packaging and gold ribbons, arranged in a professional warehouse or corporate gifting setup. Scale and abundance conveyed. Clean corporate photography, neutral background, warm lighting. No text. --ar 1:1
```
> *Dùng cho angle: social proof — nhiều doanh nghiệp tin tưởng*

### Template 2D: Ads — Sản phẩm nổi bật với text space
```
Premium Vietnamese gift box photographed from above on a white marble surface, right-aligned composition leaving left 40% of frame empty for text overlay. Wine bottle, premium crackers, artisan items visible. Clean product photography, soft shadows. Designed for advertising with copy space. No text in image. --ar 1:1
```
> *Dùng khi cần khoảng trống để overlay text trong Ads Manager*
